Privacy Policy for DailySpark Mobile Application

Effective Date: August 11, 2026

Entity: Aegis Nexus LLC ("Company", "we", "us", or "our")

Aegis Nexus LLC is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how personal and technical information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use our mobile application, DailySpark, and associated web gateways.

1. Information We Collect

To provide core mobile features, offline module persistence, and subscription management, DailySpark collects limited data categories:

  • Device Identifiers: Anonymous hardware identifiers (e.g., ANDROID_ID) collected strictly for license validation, device enrollment, and fraud prevention.
  • App Performance & Usage Metrics: Local module progress, completion timestamps, and active streak counts used to present your daily curriculum and maintain app state.
  • Financial & Transaction Data: Subscription purchase status ($4.99/mo) managed directly through Google Play In-App Billing. Aegis Nexus LLC does not collect, process, or store credit card numbers or financial account credentials.

2. How Information Is Used

Collected data is used strictly for operational and functional purposes:

  • To deliver daily educational modules, Sentry briefings, and interactive visual exercises.
  • To verify active subscription entitlements via Google Play Billing services.
  • To ensure app security, prevent unauthorized licensing, and maintain server gateway compatibility.

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal or device data to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

3. Data Encryption & Security

All network interactions between DailySpark and our secure API gateways (e.g., api.aegisnexus.ai) are transmitted over industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2+) using HTTPS encryption. Local progress statistics remain stored in secure on-device application memory.

4. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

We retain technical usage data only as long as necessary to maintain active subscriber accounts and fulfill legal compliance obligations.

You have the right to request the deletion of your DailySpark account, profile data, and associated telemetry. To initiate account and data deletion:

  • By Email: Send a deletion request to privacy@aegisnexus.ai using the Google account or email associated with your DailySpark profile.
  • In-App Support: Submit a removal request directly through the app support menu.

Retention & Purge Timeline: Upon receiving a valid deletion request, your account identifier, nickname records, and usage metrics are permanently purged from our active gateway databases within 90 days. Certain transactional records required by law or Google Play Billing financial compliance may be retained for accounting purposes.

5. Children's Privacy

DailySpark is designed as a digital safety and cognitive engagement application for general adult audiences and senior citizens. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

Operational Guidelines

Can a family member or Designated Observer fund the subscription?

Yes. We support family-funded setups. An adult child, family member, or designated advocate can manage subscriptions and billing directly, ensuring the user receives an uninterrupted, dignified experience.

How does DailySpark address emerging AI and modern scam tactics?

Scammers increasingly use AI voice cloning, realistic search engine impersonation, and synthetic verification traps. Our curriculum is actively refreshed with recent, real-world events—giving users safe, hands-on practice in spotting deceptive patterns.

Can I add a companion cellphone to my profile?

Yes. Our provisioned Companion Phone serves as an essential calling device or as a secure mobile hotspot for care facilities lacking reliable Wi-Fi. Full Sentinel behavioral interception runs inside dedicated profiles on managed tablets.

Why do you prefer provisioned hardware over BYOD?

Our provisioned hardware ecosystems are completely hardened at the OS kernel and network layer before shipment. While Bring Your Own Device (BYOD) is supported, it lacks the immediate, plug-and-play perimeter insulation of a pre-built Aegis device.

Device Ecosystem & BYOD Compatibility

Aegis Nexus profiles and behavioral protections are engineered specifically for Samsung Galaxy devices to leverage Samsung Knox hardware isolation and Android Enterprise Work Profile boundaries. We do not support Apple (iOS/iPadOS), FireOS, or generic Android devices. For Pathway 3 (BYOD), supported devices include Samsung Galaxy Tab A-series hardware running Android 13 or newer with active enterprise security maintenance. For external reference on enterprise-compatible Samsung hardware, consult the Android Enterprise Solutions Directory.

Cellular Connectivity (BYOS — Bring Your Own SIM)

All cellular connectivity across Aegis devices operates strictly on a Bring Your Own SIM (BYOS) basis. Customers supply their own active Nano-SIM or carrier-supported eSIM profile from their existing family plan or preferred provider. For environments lacking reliable home Wi-Fi—such as care homes or assisted living facilities—the Companion Phone can function as a dedicated, secured mobile hotspot to tether data to the tablet. The Executive Shield tablet also accepts a direct BYOS SIM. Aegis Nexus LLC is not a telecommunications or internet service provider and assumes no responsibility for carrier data caps, roaming charges, network outages, or signal coverage limitations.

Aegis Sentinel Scope & Best-Effort Defense

Aegis Sentinel is an assistive behavioral intent monitor and safety net designed to help identify deceptive interaction patterns, known exploit triggers, and social engineering indicators. Sentinel operates on a best-effort defense model. It is not an antivirus, Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR) platform, or absolute barrier against all malicious activity, novel zero-day threats, or operator error. Subscribers maintain responsibility for account credentials, financial authorizations, and device use.

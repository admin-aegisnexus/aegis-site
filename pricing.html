Operational Guidelines

Can a family member or caregiver fund the subscription?

Yes. We specialize in family-funded infrastructure. An adult child or legal guardian can manage the billing registry entirely via direct invoice, while the end-user receives the secure, zero-tracking device.

Can I add a companion cellphone to my profile?

Absolutely. We offer a Companion Phone Add-on. This allows secure access profiles to extend to your personal smartphone for mobile compute and synchronization alongside your core tablet architecture.

Why do you prefer provisioned hardware?

Our secure tablets are locked down at the system layer via MDM and custom web filters before delivery. Bring Your Own Device (BYOD) setups are supported but lack the unified, plug-and-play network isolation of our provisioned fleet.

Operational Guidelines

Can a family member or caregiver fund the subscription?

Yes. We specialize in family-funded infrastructure. An adult child or legal guardian can manage the billing registry entirely via direct invoice, while the end-user receives the secure, zero-tracking device.

Can I add a companion cellphone to my profile?

Absolutely. We offer a Companion Phone Add-on layout. This allows secure access profiles to extend to your personal smartphone for mobile compute and verification synchronization alongside your core tablet architecture.

Why do you prefer provisioned hardware over BYOD?

Our provisioned hardware ecosystems are completely hardened at the OS kernel and network layer before shipment. While Bring Your Own Device (BYOD) is supported, it lacks the immediate, plug-and-play perimeter insulation of a pre-built Aegis device.

Assistive behavioral intent monitoring for managed Aegis devices. Sentinel acts as a safety net against digital coercion, flagging unauthorized remote-access triggers (like AnyDesk prompts) and deceptive browser redirects before damage occurs.

A guided interface for your daily routine. DailySpark helps users build sharp defense habits through daily modules designed to reinforce scam pattern recognition, reaction speed, and digital safety.

Aegis Sentinel Scope & Best-Effort Defense

Aegis Sentinel is an assistive behavioral intent monitor and safety net designed to help identify deceptive interaction patterns, known exploit triggers, and social engineering indicators. Sentinel operates on a best-effort defense model. It is not an antivirus, Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR) platform, or absolute barrier against all malicious activity, novel zero-day threats, or operator error. Subscribers maintain responsibility for account credentials, financial authorizations, and device use.
